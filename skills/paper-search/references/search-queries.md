# PaperDB Search Query Reference

Adapt these starting points to the topic and source. Search both English
academic sources and Chinese broker-report sources.

## English academic queries

```text
(strategy OR portfolio OR timing OR stock selection) AND
(backtest OR annualized return OR Sharpe OR transaction cost) AND
(Chinese stock OR China OR A-share)
```

```text
(factor OR signal OR anomaly) AND
(formula OR construction) AND backtest AND
(Chinese stock OR China OR A-share)
```

```text

(Chinese stock OR China OR A-share)
```

## Ranking rules

Prefer established broker reports and academic papers. Blogs and community
posts may help discovery but have weaker provenance. Ingest only when title,
source, institution or authors, date, and URL can be identified reliably.

Search aggregators may provide useful leads, but retain the originating
publisher and direct PDF URL whenever available.
